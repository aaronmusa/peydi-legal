Peydi

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Peydi does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. I can’t see your accounts, your transactions or anything else you put into the app.

What the app stores

Peydi saves the following on your iPhone:

  • Your budget — households, accounts and their currencies, categories, pay cycles, envelopes, recurring bills and reminders.
  • Transactions — amounts, dates, merchants, notes, the account and category, and the exchange rate you entered for anything in another currency.
  • Receipt images — if you attach a receipt to an entry, the image and a small thumbnail are saved with it. You can delete the image and keep the entry.
  • Family support recipients and debts — the names you type in, how you send to them, and the amounts involved. These are whatever you choose to enter.
  • What the app has learned — which category and account you use for a merchant, so the next entry fills itself in.
  • Preferences — your default account, the Face ID lock, whether you budget by payday, and whether iCloud sync is on.

Typing, speaking and scanning

When you log something like “jollibee 250”, Peydi reads it on your iPhone. When the built-in rules can’t place an entry, it asks Apple’s on-device language model, which also runs entirely on your iPhone. Receipts you scan with the camera or share to Peydi are read with Apple’s on-device text recognition. None of this text or imagery is sent to me or to anyone else.

If you set up a Shortcuts automation that passes a bank or e-wallet text message to Peydi, that text is read on your iPhone in the same way. The automation is yours: Peydi can’t read your messages on its own.

If you log with Siri, Apple processes your voice request under Apple’s own privacy policy before handing Peydi the words.

On your iPhone

Bill reminders, budget alerts and the reminder before a free trial ends are scheduled on your iPhone as local notifications; no push server is involved. Entries are added to your iPhone’s own Spotlight index so you can search for them, and the widgets, Live Activity and Siri read from the app’s storage on the same phone.

CSV and PDF exports, and the sync log in Settings, are created on your iPhone and only leave it if you share them.

iCloud

If you’re signed in to iCloud and leave sync on, your data is mirrored to your own private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices and survives a new phone. That database belongs to your Apple Account. It is not a server of mine, and I have no access to it — Apple does not give developers the ability to read a user’s private CloudKit data.

You can choose local-only mode when you set Peydi up. Your data then stays on that iPhone until you turn sync on.

Sharing with a partner

If you create a shared household and invite someone, that household’s accounts, envelopes and transactions are stored in a shared iCloud zone that belongs to your Apple Account. Apple makes it available to the people you invite, and each entry shows who logged or last edited it, using the name each person chose. Your personal household is never shared. You can stop sharing at any time, which removes the other person’s access. I can’t read shared data either.

Purchases

Peydi Pro is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store, as a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple tells the app whether Pro is active; it does not tell me who you are. I never see your name, payment details or Apple Account.

In a shared household, one member’s Pro unlocks it for everyone in it. To do that, Peydi saves which member’s Pro covers the household and when it ends, in the household’s shared iCloud zone, where the other members can see it.

What the app does not do

  • No bank connections. Peydi never asks for bank or e-wallet logins.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party libraries that phone home. The app’s only network use is Apple’s own iCloud and App Store services.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.

Children

Peydi is a budgeting app for adults and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. To remove data mirrored to iCloud, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage and delete Peydi’s data. You can also delete individual entries inside the app at any time. If you own a shared household, deleting it removes it for everyone in it.

Changes
