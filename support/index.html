Reporting a problem

The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • What you were doing, and what you expected instead.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › General › About).
  • The Peydi version, shown at the bottom of Peydi’s Settings.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

If Peydi read an entry wrong, telling me exactly what you typed usually narrows it down straight away.

Common questions

I bought Pro and it hasn’t appeared. Open Settings in Peydi, tap Peydi Pro, then Restore purchases at the bottom of the screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you’re signed in to the same one you bought with. If it still doesn’t appear, email me.

Is Pro a subscription? You can choose monthly, annual, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings, and one Pro purchase covers both people in a shared household.

My entries aren’t showing on my other iPhone. Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on, and Peydi must not be in local-only mode. Peydi works offline, so entries made without a connection sync once one returns. Settings in Peydi shows the current sync state.

My partner can’t see our shared household. Both of you need iCloud switched on, and your partner needs to accept the invite link on their iPhone. Your personal accounts are never shared.

Where do exchange rates come from? You enter the rate when you log something in another currency, and Peydi remembers the last rate for each currency pair. It never fetches rates over the network.

How do I delete my data? Deleting the app removes everything on that device. To clear what’s mirrored to iCloud, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage and delete Peydi’s data. Individual entries can be swiped away inside the app.
