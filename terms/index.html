Using Peydi

Peydi is free to download and use. Peydi Pro

Peydi Pro unlocks unlimited accounts, envelopes and budget alerts, debts, every report period with comparisons and the PDF summary, receipt import, and sharing a household with your partner. It’s offered as:

Family Sharing and shared households
